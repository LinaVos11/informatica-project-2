A little more about us

Welcome to SongSip

SongSip is a website made to help you discover a new song every day. We believe that every song has a story waiting to be heard, and maybe one of those stories will become part of yours.

Every day, you choose the genre you want to explore. Whether you're in the mood for pop, rock, indie, country, R&B, alternative, or something completely different, there's always something new waiting for you.

With so many songs in the world, it's easy to keep listening to the same playlists and the same favourites. SongSip is here to help you step outside your usual music bubble and discover something you might never have found on your own. You don't have to love every song. Maybe you'll find a new favourite artist, discover a song that perfectly matches your mood, or simply hear a story you've never heard before.

SongSip is designed to connect with your Spotify account and check whether a recommended song is already one of your favourites.

So, when you're ready, click the button and discover your new song.
